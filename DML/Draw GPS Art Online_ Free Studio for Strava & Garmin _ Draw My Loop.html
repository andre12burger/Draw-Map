GPS Art Studio: Draw Artistic Routes on the Map

Create GPS art for running and cycling right in your browser. Draw My Loop is the simplest GPS art route editor: draw shapes on the map, snap them to real roads in one click, and export a GPX file ready to follow.

How to create GPS art online

1

Choose a shape

Pick from our shape library (animals, letters, symbols) or draw freehand on the map. You can also project a logo or text, then turn on Free mode if you need to preserve an off-road trace.

2

Snap to roads

Our snapping algorithm fits your drawing to real roads and paths. Review the route and local access conditions before heading out.

3

Export and run

Download your GPX file and import it into Strava, Garmin Connect, Komoot or your GPS watch. Your recorded track will form the artwork.

Compatible with your devices

Export as GPX and send the route to Strava, Garmin, Komoot, Suunto, Coros or another GPX-compatible app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Studio free?

Yes. Drawing, shape projection, road snapping and GPX export are free. A free account is required for GPX export; Auto-Fit is an optional paid feature.

Does it work worldwide?

Yes, Draw My Loop uses OpenStreetMap data which covers the entire world. You can create GPS art in any city.

How do I import my route into Strava?

Export your route as GPX, then in Strava go to Routes > Create Route > Import and select the file.
